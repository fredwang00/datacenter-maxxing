Datacenter Economics: A Year-by-Year Simulation

Supply ceilings, capital, and diffusion — simulated year by year, not fit to the source forecast

Four final uses determine demand: frontier research, commercial inference, enterprise internal use, and sovereign workloads. Economic adoption, provider revenue, and compute spending are separate assumptions. Owners and rental intermediaries describe the same capacity, not additional demand.

Segment splits, budgets, adoption, and routing are illustrative and have not been recalibrated. Source comparisons below remain visible; they are not targets this version is tuned to hit. The provider-revenue diagnostic uses a fixed seed (12345). Deployment migration and workload efficiency use illustrative assumptions. GW is IT capacity. Funding guarantees and regional constraints remain outside this update.
