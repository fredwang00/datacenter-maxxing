Datacenter Economics: A Year-by-Year Simulation

Supply ceilings, capital, and diffusion — simulated year by year, not fit to the source forecast

This model deliberately disagrees with its source in several places, most notably 2029 GW landing around 23 against a stated 95, and 2028 lab share landing around 52% against a stated 70–80% — because capital and diffusion ceilings bind before those targets are reached. Those two are findings, not errors; the full picture, including compute price and cumulative capex/credit, is in Calibration vs. source below, which states plainly how many targets — and how many of the out-of-sample validators — actually pass.

The 2029 figure is a single seed draw (12345), not a distribution. Across seeds 1–40 it ranges 22.7–76.2 GW (3.36×) while the 2026–28 path holds for 31 of 40 draws and none reach the stated 95 — the direction (a monetization-driven shortfall) is robust; the exact magnitude is seed-sensitive.
